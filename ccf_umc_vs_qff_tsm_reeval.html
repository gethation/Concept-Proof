CCF/UMC vs QFF/TSM — re-evaluation

Common window 2026-07-06 → 08-12, executable-book pricing, 1M TWD per leg on 2M capital. Every figure recomputed from data/processed at build time.

The point estimate favours CCF/UMC roughly 2:1. The statistics do not support calling it a win.

On 25 overlapping session-days the Sharpe gap is +5.44 — but its 95% bootstrap interval is [-3.85, +14.04], which straddles zero, and QFF/TSM is the better pair in 11% of resamples. Five weeks and 23/17 trades cannot separate two strategies this volatile. What is well-evidenced is the robustness gap below — that claim rests on 100 cells per pair, not on one.

Where QFF/TSM is genuinely better

Microstructure headroom. Median edge is 21.6 QFF ticks against CCF/UMC's 2.5. After the 2026-07-05 tick change (5 TWD → 1 TWD) QFF trades far clear of its own quantum, while CCF still works right at its tick — CCF/UMC's result depends on the book model in a way QFF/TSM's does not.

Risk symmetry. 1 of 17 QFF/TSM holds span a weekend against 6 of 23 for CCF/UMC, and CCF/UMC sits in the market 79% of the time against 46%. The Monday TAIFEX-open-before-NYSE window — where the CCF leg moves unhedged and the strategy cannot exit — is a real cost the backtest cannot see.

Diversification. Daily-return correlation is +0.080. A 50/50 blend Sharpes 10.82 against CCF/UMC's 10.75 alone. QFF/TSM's case is as a second, uncorrelated book — not as a standalone replacement.

Recommended CCF/UMC configuration

The grid's raw Sharpe winner is w390 / entry 2.0 / exit 0.5 at Sharpe 9.73 — and it is unusable: median edge 1.40 ticks with 40% of trades inside a single tick. That is bid-ask bounce, even after the displacement charge. Screening on per-trade margin, trade count and neighbourhood stability leaves 7 of 100 cells.

Which survivor holds up if the book is wider than measured

Best Sharpe among the screened survivors (8.88), the highest net (214,508 TWD), the most trades of any survivor (23), only 9% of trades inside one tick — and it is the only candidate whose Sharpe is essentially flat out to a book 39% wider than measured (8.88 → 8.75 at displacement 0.30, still 5.56 at 0.43, double the measured width). Its rivals lose a third to a half of their Sharpe over the same range.

This re-evaluation lands on the same cell the 2026-08-13 comparison chose, but for a better reason: not because it topped the Sharpe ranking, but because it is the one that survives the screens and the cost-model stress.

Before trusting any of this

23 trades over 26 session-days. The 96% win rate and profit factor near 40 are small-sample artifacts, not forward estimates. Nothing here is out-of-sample: the parameters were chosen on the same 5 weeks they are scored on. Six of the 23 trades carry a weekend, and the Monday unhedged-margin window that implies has been measured to breach maintenance margin on 3 of 4 weekend holds in earlier work — funding must clear roughly 25% of leg notional before this is live-safe. The honest read is a promising configuration with a well-understood cost model, awaiting out-of-sample evidence.
